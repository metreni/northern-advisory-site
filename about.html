About

An independent practice,
led by a CPA.

Northern Advisory brings the financial judgment of private equity and real estate development to owner-operated businesses, investors, and developers.

Matthew Etreni, CPA

Matthew founded Northern Advisory in 2026 after seven years as a CPA in private equity and real estate development finance. That work meant building the models behind acquisitions and refinancings, preparing the reporting that lenders and investors rely on, and keeping multi-entity structures compliant.

He started the practice because most small businesses and investors are offered one of two things: bookkeeping and filings with little judgment behind them, or advice that costs more than it saves. Northern Advisory sits between the two, with the same person doing the work and answering the questions.

Every engagement is handled by Matthew directly. The practice takes on a limited number of clients at a time so that each one gets that attention.
