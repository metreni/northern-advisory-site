Most business owners reviewing operating performance prefer a direct statement of cash flows. The reason is usually simplicity. An indirect statement of cash flows is often treated as a compliance checklist item rather than a meaningful report.

Northern Advisory is here to challenge that assumption. With some modifications to the reporting, an indirect statement of cash flows can be more insightful and relevant for business owners than a direct cash flow. It can answer questions like:

1. How much in revenue did we bill this month? And how much by stream?
2. How much in revenue did we collect this month?
3. How much of our expenses were incurred on credit?
4. How much of our expenses were paid in cash?
