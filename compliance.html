2.2

Partnership Return (T5013)

Preparation and filing of partnership information returns with the CRA, including allocation of income or loss to each partner and T5013 slips. Common across the LP/GP and co-ownership structures we serve.

What's included

  • Filing assessment: whether the partnership must file (size thresholds, corporate/trust partners, tiered partnerships)
  • T5013 return and schedules, prepared from year-end figures
  • Allocation of net income or loss to each partner per the partnership agreement
  • T5013 slips prepared and distributed to every partner
  • Coordination with each partner's T1, T2, or T3 so the reported shares agree

What's not included

  • Each partner's own return (T1, T2, or T3), billed separately
  • Partnership agreement drafting or restructuring (Advisory)
  • Financial statements (Reporting)

Pricing

StandardOperating partnership over $2M in revenue+expenses or $5M in assets; allocations and slips$1,800

Filed by the partnership's due date; T5013 slips distributed to every partner. Partner-level filings (each partner's T1, T2, or T3) are billed separately. All fees plus HST.

2.3

Trust Return (T3)

Preparation and filing of trust income tax and information returns with the CRA, including beneficiary allocations, T3 slips, and beneficial-ownership disclosure. Covers family trusts, holding trusts, and estates.

What's included

  • T3 return and schedules, including Schedule 15 (beneficial ownership) where required
  • Allocation of income and capital to beneficiaries
  • T3 slips and T3 Summary preparation and filing
  • Routine assessment follow-up

What's not included

  • Trust deed drafting or restructuring (referred to legal counsel)
  • Proactive tax planning (an Advisory retainer)
  • Audits and objections (see 2.7)
