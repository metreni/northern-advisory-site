1.1

Bookkeeping

The recording of your transactions into a double-entry accounting system, with monthly reconciliations and a trial balance, on a fixed monthly fee. GnuCash is available as Northern Advisory's default accounting system.

What's included

  • Transaction entry from source documents and bank/credit-card statements
  • Bank and credit-card reconciliations
  • Month-end close and trial balance
  • Standard account reconciliations as agreed (intercompany, loans, prepaids)
  • Accounting records maintained in the agreed accounting system and standard reporting

What's not included

  • Payroll runs (remittances and T4s are a Compliance service)
  • Bill payment and accounts payable/receivable administration
  • GST/HST filing and tax returns
  • Financial statements (1.2) and CSRS 4200 compilation (1.3)

What we need from you

  • Monthly bank and credit-card statements (or portal access)
  • Source documents, complete and timely
  • Confirmation of intercompany / related-party transactions
  • Responses to queries within 3 business days
  • Access to your preferred accounting system, at no software cost to Northern Advisory (GnuCash is available as Northern Advisory's default system)

1.2

Internal Financial Statements

Balance sheet, income statement, statement of cash flows, and statement of changes in equity, prepared from your books for internal management use.

What's included

  • Balance sheet, income statement, statement of cash flows, statement of changes in equity
  • Summary notes as agreed
  • Prepared from closed books (bookkeeping clients) or your trial balance
  • Pairs with management reporting (1.4)

What's not included

  • Compilation engagement report (see 1.3) or any assurance
  • Full financial-statement notes beyond the summary agreed
  • Tax returns and provisions
  • Statements for third-party external use

1.3

CSRS 4200 Compilation

Annual financial statements compiled in accordance with CSRS 4200, Compilation Engagements, including applicable notes and a compilation engagement report.

What's included

  • Year-end adjusting entries and finalization of the trial balance
  • Financial statements with applicable notes
  • Compilation engagement report
  • Review of significant balances with management
  • Signed PDF statements + working file

What's not included

  • Assurance (audit or review)
  • Tax returns, provisions, or tax planning
  • Internal statements during the year (see 1.2), unless separately scoped

1.4

Management Reporting

KPI and special-purpose reporting: a specific metric or view you need tracked that isn't already obvious in the financial statements (e.g., variance vs. budget, unit economics, project tracking).

What's included

  • Design of the report format and metric definitions
  • Periodic production (typically monthly)
  • Variance vs. budget analysis where a budget exists
  • Brief written commentary on what the numbers show

What's not included

  • Creation of the budget or forecast itself (an Advisory service)
  • Financial statement preparation (see 1.2)
  • Data sourcing beyond what you provide
